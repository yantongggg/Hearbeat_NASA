      HEARTHBEAT

      Hear how home is doing.

      Astronauts can see home from orbit, but cannot hear it. A student who cannot see is shown home only as colour maps.

      Hearthbeat turns NASA's Earth data into sound. Choose your home and hear it: Is it raining? Is the air clean? How much has it warmed?

      Made first for people who cannot read a colour map. At least 2.2 billion people live with some vision impairment (WHO). Best with headphones.
